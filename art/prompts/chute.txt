Use case: the prize drop chute of a claw machine for a 2D mobile game ad, to be placed into the floor shelf at the bottom of the machine window.
Subject: a single glossy chute bezel seen from the front with a slight downward tilt (about 30 degrees from above): a thick polished ring made of an outer chrome lip and an inner orange band with soft highlights, shaped as a wide ellipse about 1.7 times wider than tall, with a short rounded front lip at the bottom. The opening inside the ring is filled with flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF (nothing inside it: no depth, no gradient, no shadow). Two small cream round light bulbs are set into the bezel at its left and right ends. Soft clay/vinyl render, crisp highlights, warm studio light from the upper left.
Composition: one object centred, filling about 80% of the canvas width, flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background everywhere else.
Constraints: exactly one chute, no text, no letters, no logos, no shadows on the background, no glow outside the ring.
