Use case: four appetising food items for a mobile playable ad, to be placed inside glass capsules.
Subject: four separate unbranded fast-food items in one horizontal row, left to right: (1) a juicy cheeseburger with sesame bun, lettuce, tomato slice, melted cheese and a thick patty, seen from the front with a slight downward tilt; (2) a plain red-and-cream paper carton full of golden french fries, seen from the front; (3) a tall lidded soda cup with a straw and a plain red band, no label, no logo; (4) a small pile of four golden chicken nuggets with a tiny dipping sauce cup. Glossy soft-3D render, rich saturated colours, soft studio light from the upper left.
Composition: one horizontal row, the four items the same visual weight and similar height, each centred in its quarter of the canvas with very wide empty gaps (at least 15% of the canvas width) between them, nothing touching, generous margin to the edges, flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background everywhere else.
Constraints: exactly four items, no text, no letters, no logos, no real brands, no plates, no table, no shadows on the background.
