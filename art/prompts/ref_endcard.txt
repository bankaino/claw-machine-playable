Use case: concept end card of a mobile playable ad, vertical 2:3, reward screen after winning.
Subject: a celebratory offer screen on a deep forest-green gradient background with soft orange bokeh circles and a few cream and orange confetti pieces. Centre hero: a glossy round serving tray seen slightly from above holding a cheeseburger, a paper box of french fries, a tall lidded soda cup with a straw and a stack of chicken nuggets with a dipping sauce cup, all unbranded, appetising and big. A round tomato-red price badge with a cream rim overlaps the top-right of the tray (blank bars instead of digits). Above: a blank cream rounded bar as a headline. Below the tray: a promo-code card in cream with a dashed orange border, a blank dark bar for the code and a small orange rounded 'copy' button beside it (no text). At the bottom a large orange glossy rounded call-to-action button (blank), and below it two thin lines of blank grey bars as fine print.
Composition: portrait 2:3, tray in the upper-middle about 40% of height, everything centred and symmetric.
Constraints: NO text, NO letters, NO digits, NO logos, no real brand, no phone frame, no watermark. Food and cup are unbranded.
