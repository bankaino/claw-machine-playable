Use case: concept key frame of a mobile playable ad, vertical 2:3, the exciting core moment.
Subject: the same modern claw machine as the first frame, the WHOLE machine visible with a small margin of blurred background on both sides (nothing cropped): deep forest-green glossy body, orange trim, small cream round lights down both sides, slim blank orange sign bar on top. The glass window is the dominant element: a very tall, wide, warm golden-lit glass play area filling about 62% of the frame height and almost the full width, with soft diagonal reflections. Mid-play: the chrome claw has closed around one SMALL glossy transparent capsule sphere holding a cheeseburger and is lifting it up through a lot of empty golden space, a few cream sparkles around it; the capsule is about one sixth of the window width. On the floor, a thin low pile with the other three SMALL capsules spaced apart (french fries box, lidded soda cup with straw, chicken nuggets) among a shallow layer of tiny orange, red and cream balls; a large clear gap between the lifted capsule and the floor pile. Above the window a slim row of four small round slots: the first filled and glowing with a cheeseburger icon, the other three dim silhouettes; a small timer pill glowing red-orange (blank bars). Below the window a SLIM, LOW control deck taking only about 13% of the frame height: medium glossy orange push button with a claw icon on the left, chrome lever with a red ball knob tilted right on the right. At the bottom a thin orange rounded bar (blank) as a call-to-action button.
Composition: portrait 2:3, machine centred and symmetric, play window clearly the largest part of the picture, soft blurred deep-green background with orange bokeh circles.
Constraints: NO text, NO letters, NO digits, NO logos, no real brand, no phone frame, no watermark. Food and cup are unbranded.
