Use case: concept key frame of a mobile playable ad, vertical 2:3, first screen the viewer sees.
Subject: a modern claw machine seen straight-on, the WHOLE machine visible with a small margin of blurred background on both sides (nothing cropped). Deep forest-green glossy body with orange trim and a column of small cream round lights along both sides; a slim blank orange sign bar on top (no text). The glass window is the dominant element: a very tall, wide, warm golden-lit glass play area filling about 62% of the frame height and almost the full width of the machine, with soft diagonal reflections. Inside the glass: a chrome claw hanging from the top rail on a thin cable, open, with a LARGE EMPTY GOLDEN SPACE below it (roughly half of the window height is empty air between the claw and the floor, the claw is far away from everything). On the floor, only a thin low pile: four SMALL glossy transparent capsule spheres spaced apart in a row, each about one sixth of the window width, each holding a tiny appetising food item (cheeseburger, box of french fries, lidded soda cup with straw, stack of chicken nuggets), surrounded by a shallow layer of tiny orange, red and cream balls. Above the window a slim row of four small round slots (dim food silhouettes) and a small timer pill (blank bars). Below the window a SLIM, LOW control deck taking only about 13% of the frame height: on the left a medium round glossy orange push button with a claw icon, on the right a chrome lever with a red ball knob and two small arrow triangles; a cartoon white-gloved hand grips the lever knob. At the very bottom a thin orange rounded bar (blank, no text) as a call-to-action button.
Composition: portrait 2:3, machine centred and symmetric, play window clearly the largest part of the picture, soft blurred deep-green background with orange bokeh circles.
Constraints: NO text, NO letters, NO digits, NO logos, no real brand, no phone frame, no watermark. Food and cup are unbranded.
