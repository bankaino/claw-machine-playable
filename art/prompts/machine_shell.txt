Subject: the empty claw machine cabinet seen exactly straight-on, symmetric, filling the canvas width with a small margin. Glossy deep-green body #0F4A35 with orange #FF7A1A trim, soft clay/vinyl render look, crisp highlights, soft inner shading.
Composition: portrait 2:3, perfectly symmetric, the cabinet touches nearly the left and right edges; everything outside the cabinet silhouette AND everything inside the window opening is the same flat pure magenta #FF00FF.
Constraints: NO text, NO letters, NO digits, no logos, no real brand, no phone frame, no shadow on the background, no glow outside the cabinet.
