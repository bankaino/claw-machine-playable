Use case: parts of a chrome claw for a claw machine in a 2D mobile game, to be assembled and rotated by code.
Subject: two separate parts in one horizontal row, left to right: (1) the claw HEAD: a short glossy chrome hub block with a cable clamp on top (a little ring where a cable attaches) and two small round pivot knobs, one on each side near the bottom, perfectly left-right symmetric, front view; (2) one claw FINGER: a single slim curved chrome prong hanging straight down, with a round pivot eye at its very top and a gently curved pointed tip bending inward at the bottom, front view, vertical. Glossy polished steel with soft highlights, a hint of cool blue reflection, soft clay/vinyl render look.
Composition: one horizontal row, each part centred in its half of the canvas with a very wide gap (at least 20% of the canvas width) between them, nothing touching, generous margin, flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background everywhere else.
Constraints: exactly two parts, no cable, no text, no logos, no shadows on the background.
